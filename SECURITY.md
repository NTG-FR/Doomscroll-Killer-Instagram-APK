# Security policy

Doomscroll Killer handles a logged-in Instagram session and uses an accessibility service and notification access, so security reports are taken seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private [security advisory form](https://github.com/NTG-FR/Doomscroll-Killer-Instagram-APK/security/advisories/new) instead, with:

- what the problem is and its impact,
- steps to reproduce,
- the app version and Android version.

You should get a first answer within a week.

## Scope

In scope: anything that could expose the Instagram session, let another app or website abuse the app's permissions, or make the app send data off the device.

Out of scope: Instagram's own website or app, and issues requiring a rooted or already-compromised device.

## Only install official builds

Download the APK from this repository's [Releases](https://github.com/NTG-FR/Doomscroll-Killer-Instagram-APK/releases) only. A modified copy distributed elsewhere could steal your Instagram session.
